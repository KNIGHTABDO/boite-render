# Ep07 (Chapitre VII): direction plan

Source: `source/boite.txt` lines 1819–2285 (7.1k words, 40 % longer than ch. VI).
YouTube title: **« Chapitre 7 — La nuit de l'Achoura »**. The search keyword comes first: *La Boîte à merveilles chapitre 7 résumé*.
Rules: `../ep06/POSTMORTEM.md` §4 (R1–R15) + WORKFLOW-V2 §2.
Target **13:30, hard cap 14:00**. That is about 1,800 spoken words, so we narrate about 25 % of the text; the rest is told in images and sound.

## The idea (one line)
A child counts down to the most beautiful night of his life. Between him and that night stands a razor.
**Light** and **sound** carry the whole chapter.

## Director's devices (each one has a job)

| device | where | why |
|---|---|---|
| **Non-linear cold open** (start near the barber's climax, cut on the blade) | 0:00–0:14 | Leaves a question the viewer must stay to answer. First conflict at second 1, not 1:54. |
| **Ticking motif**: the razor stroked on the oily stone, *shhk… shhk…* | cold open; at "je redoutais Si Abderrahman" (night J-2); the dream; the barber | Nolan's clock. Every return tightens the dread before the payoff. |
| **Time cards** (J-2 / LA VEILLE / LA NUIT / LE JOUR DE L'ACHOURA) burned over action | 4 act starts | Dunkirk-style chronology. It is also exactly the countdown students are tested on. |
| **Light arc**: dry lamp → candle stub → lamp → glasses of oil in the Msid → façade *féerie* → lights dying at dawn → lamp lit at the end, nobody has any spirit left → black | through the episode | The image of joy rising and falling. The episode ends on the light going out over the Boîte. |
| **Sound arc**: instruments as the score; music held back until the façade reveal | through the episode | Music is a reward. The first score cue is the façade (~9:00). |
| **Subjective sound** | the muezzin he can't hear; the barber's voice turning into a drone as he falls asleep; whispering *présences* on the dark stairs | We are inside a 6-year-old's head. |
| **Set-up → pay-off**: the *hymne à la rue* ↔ "jamais la rue ne me parut aussi belle" | barber | The barber's poem plays over the street images; when the boy walks out, he lives it. |
| **Absence as reveal**: the Maître **without his rod** | Msid dawn | First time ever. Framed like the end of a war. |
| **Plants for later chapters**: Moulay Larbi "should take a new wife"; Aicha's "Ils sont… Ils sont…" | barber; Aicha | Hard silence plus an "À retenir" tag. Students meet these again in ch. VIII–IX. |
| **Cross-cut, one sound over two spaces**: his barbaric drumming on the terrace ↔ the women whispering secrets below | Aicha visit | The child's noise covers the adults' secret. |
| **Audio-driven light**: in the Msid the flames flicker with the pupils' chant (light intensity from the chant's envelope) | féerie | "Les flammes psalmodiaient", shown literally. Image and sound are one. |

## Sound plan
- **Foley library first** (`audio/foley/`, CC0, reused every episode):
  - instruments: bendir, tambourine, tin trumpet, hourglass clay drum
  - razor on stone, scissors, tin clicks, ladder creak, water pour, splash, scrub
  - cat yowl, cane tapping, footsteps on stone and stairs, door
  - incense crackle, lamp hiss
  - crowd walla (souk / pupils), women's laughter, kids cheering, sparrows, muezzin
- **Cue sheet** from every sound word in the script (R9).
- **Music:** only (1) the cold-open drone, (2) the façade reveal + chant, (3) the fiche. Everything else is diegetic.
- **Voices:**
  - Kanza gets a female voice.
  - Zoubida's and Kanza's off-screen calls get distance and reverb.
  - The chant and cantiques should be **real singing**, not TTS: a sung TTS test first, then a Lyria/Gemini option.
  - The barber needs a voice that can run fast without breathing (comic logorrhoea).
